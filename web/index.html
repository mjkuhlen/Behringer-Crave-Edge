Edge selected. The Edge's dual sequencer has no documented SysEx — pattern dump/write is Crave-only. To play the Edge from the app: ① rear MIDI CHANNEL DIP switches — all four DOWN = channel 1 (legend printed on the case); match App MIDI channel in Device Settings. ② For clean note playback, STOP the internal sequencer and set SEQ PITCH MOD = OFF (middle), then tune with the FREQUENCY knobs — otherwise the step pitch knobs fight the incoming notes. ③ To run the Edge's own sequencer instead: CLOCK switch = USB/MIDI, enable ⏱ clock mode on the device chip and press ▶ (the Edge starts on incoming clock). MIDI velocity appears at the VELOCITY CV jack (patch it to VCF MOD for velocity-driven filter movement). No MIDI swing, no MIDI sequence reset — those are panel-only.

The Crave's pots are analog (no SysEx control), so modulation reaches the patch bay via the ASSIGN jack: hit → ASSIGN on a slot to switch the ASSIGN output to that slot's source, then patch ASSIGN into VCF cutoff, VCO pitch, VCA… Only one source feeds ASSIGN at a time.
